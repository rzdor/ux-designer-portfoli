# UX Designer Portfolio - Product Requirements Document

## Core Purpose & Success

**Mission Statement**: Create a professional, visually compelling portfolio website that showcases UX design expertise and attracts potential clients and employers.

**Success Indicators**: 
- Increased inquiries from potential clients/employers
- Professional presentation of design work and process
- Clear demonstration of UX design skills and impact

**Experience Qualities**: Professional, Elegant, Intuitive

## Project Classification & Approach

**Complexity Level**: Content Showcase (information-focused with interactive elements)
**Primary User Activity**: Consuming (viewing portfolio work and designer information)

## Thought Process for Feature Selection

**Core Problem Analysis**: UX designers need a platform to effectively showcase their work, process, and professional experience to potential clients and employers.

**User Context**: Recruiters, potential clients, and peers will visit to evaluate the designer's capabilities, work quality, and professional approach.

**Critical Path**: Landing → Portfolio browsing → Project details → Contact

**Key Moments**: 
1. First impression from hero section and featured work
2. Detailed project case studies showing design process
3. Clear contact and next steps

## Essential Features

### 1. Hero Section with Professional Introduction
- **What it does**: Introduces the designer with compelling headline and brief value proposition
- **Why it matters**: Creates strong first impression and immediately communicates designer's focus
- **Success criteria**: Clear professional positioning and call-to-action visibility

### 2. Featured Project Gallery with Real Figma Designs
- **What it does**: Displays portfolio projects with actual design mockups and case study details
- **Why it matters**: Demonstrates actual design work and problem-solving approach
- **Success criteria**: Projects are visually appealing and include comprehensive case study information

### 3. Interactive Project Details Dialog
- **What it does**: Provides detailed project information including problem, solution, and results
- **Why it matters**: Shows design thinking process and impact measurement
- **Success criteria**: Clear presentation of design process and measurable outcomes

### 4. Professional About Section
- **What it does**: Communicates designer's background, skills, and approach
- **Why it matters**: Builds trust and personal connection with potential clients
- **Success criteria**: Professional yet personable representation of designer

### 5. Contact Form and Information
- **What it does**: Provides multiple ways for potential clients to get in touch
- **Why it matters**: Converts portfolio visits into business inquiries
- **Success criteria**: Low-friction contact process with clear response expectations

## Design Direction

### Visual Tone & Identity
**Emotional Response**: Trust, competence, and creative sophistication
**Design Personality**: Clean, professional, elegant with subtle creative touches
**Visual Metaphors**: Clean lines, careful spacing, and purposeful typography reflecting good design principles
**Simplicity Spectrum**: Minimal interface that lets the work speak for itself

### Color Strategy
**Color Scheme Type**: Monochromatic with warm accent
**Primary Color**: Deep charcoal (#3F3F46) - professional and sophisticated
**Secondary Colors**: Light grays for backgrounds and supporting elements
**Accent Color**: Warm copper (#C2410C) - draws attention to key actions and highlights
**Color Psychology**: Gray conveys professionalism and neutrality, copper adds warmth and creativity
**Foreground/Background Pairings**: 
- White background (#FFFFFF) with deep charcoal text (#3F3F46) - WCAG AA compliant
- Charcoal background (#3F3F46) with white text (#FFFFFF) - WCAG AA compliant
- Copper accent (#C2410C) with white text (#FFFFFF) - WCAG AA compliant

### Typography System
**Font Pairing Strategy**: Inter for all text elements providing clean, modern readability
**Typographic Hierarchy**: Clear distinction between headings (bold, larger), subheadings (medium weight), and body text
**Font Personality**: Professional, clean, highly legible - reflecting good UX design principles
**Which fonts**: Inter (Google Fonts) - excellent for screen reading and professional appearance
**Legibility Check**: Inter is specifically designed for screen legibility with excellent contrast

### Visual Hierarchy & Layout
**Attention Direction**: Large hero text → featured work grid → individual project details
**White Space Philosophy**: Generous spacing creates breathing room and focuses attention on content
**Grid System**: Responsive grid system with consistent spacing and alignment
**Responsive Approach**: Mobile-first design with progressive enhancement for larger screens

### Animations
**Purposeful Meaning**: Subtle hover effects and smooth transitions reinforce interactivity
**Hierarchy of Movement**: Gentle hover animations on interactive elements, smooth dialog transitions
**Contextual Appropriateness**: Minimal, purposeful animations that enhance rather than distract

### UI Elements & Component Selection
**Component Usage**: Cards for projects, dialogs for detailed views, badges for tags and categories
**Component States**: Clear hover states for interactive elements, focus states for accessibility
**Icon Selection**: Phosphor icons for clean, consistent iconography
**Mobile Adaptation**: Stack layout on mobile, maintain touch-friendly interaction areas

## Edge Cases & Problem Scenarios
**Potential Obstacles**: Slow image loading, various screen sizes, accessibility requirements
**Edge Case Handling**: Progressive image loading, responsive design, keyboard navigation support
**Technical Constraints**: Browser compatibility, performance on mobile devices

## Implementation Considerations
**Scalability Needs**: Easy addition of new projects, maintainable code structure
**Testing Focus**: Cross-browser compatibility, mobile responsiveness, accessibility compliance
**Critical Questions**: How will new projects be added? How will the site perform on slow connections?
